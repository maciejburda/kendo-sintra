## What we collect

If you fill in our **sign-up form**, you send us your name, date of birth, email
address and where you heard about us. That is all we ask for.

The site itself collects nothing about you and shows no ads. It loads two things
from Google — a **map** and **Google Analytics** — and both may set cookies.
Neither loads until you agree: a banner asks when you first visit, and if you
decline or ignore it, nothing is requested from Google at all. You can change
your mind from "Cookie settings" in the footer.

## Why we use it

To reply to you and to organise your training. The legal basis is your consent,
which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time
by writing to kendosintra@gmail.com.

## How long we keep it

If you train with us, for as long as you train with us plus **12 months**.
If you send the form but never start, we delete it **12 months** after we last
hear from you.

## Who else sees it

The form runs on **Google Forms** and answers are stored in **Google Sheets**,
so Google processes this data on our behalf. Nobody else receives it. We never
sell or share it.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object
to our using it — just write to kendosintra@gmail.com. You can also complain to
the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de
Dados (CNPD).

## Photographs

Photos of training and competitions appear on this site and on our social media.
We publish a photo only with the consent of the person in it, or their parent or
guardian if they are under 18. Want one taken down? Write to kendosintra@gmail.com.
