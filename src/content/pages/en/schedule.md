We train **twice a week**, on Monday and Wednesday evenings from 19:00 to 21:00, at Quinta da Beloura in Sintra.

Beginners are welcome to come and watch any training evening — you do not need to book, and you do not need to bring anything.

## Seminars and competitions

National competitions and gradings are organised by the
[Federação Portuguesa de Kendo, Iaido e Jodo](https://kendo.pt/).
