description: Monthly training fees at Kendo Club Sintra — 20 EUR for adults, 10 EUR for children and members of other Portuguese Kendo clubs.
eyebrow: Monthly membership
---

| | Per month |
|---|---|
| Adults | **20 EUR** |
| Children | **10 EUR** |
| Members of other Portuguese Kendo clubs | **10 EUR** |

There is no joining fee. The three-month beginner course is charged separately at 60 EUR in total, and includes a shinai you keep on completion.

Armour (bogu) rental plans are planned for graduates of the beginner course, so you can continue training without the initial investment in your own equipment.
