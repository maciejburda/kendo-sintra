## The dojo

We train twice a week in the sports hall at TASIS Portugal International School, at Quinta da Beloura II in Sintra. Both adults and children train with us, from complete beginners through to graded kendoka.

Classes are run in English and Portuguese, with all commands, technique names and counting in Japanese — as kendo is taught everywhere.

## Affiliation

We are a member club of the **Federação Portuguesa de Kendo, Iaido e Jodo (FPKIJ)**,
the national body representing kendo in Portugal, which is in turn recognised by the
European Kendo Federation. The federation lists us at [kendo.pt](https://kendo.pt/clubes/).

## Grades and competition

Our members grade and compete internationally. In 2026 our youth members took results at the Konotori Cup in The Hague, and Marij and Pedro became the first kendoka to reach 1st kyu having started their journey at our dojo from the very beginning.

Our team also took **bronze at the Portuguese National Club Championships**.
